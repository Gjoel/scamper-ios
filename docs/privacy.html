Scamper privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Scamper is an app for learning Latin American Spanish. We built it so that it does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Scamper has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send any information over the internet.

Your progress

Your learning progress, XP, coins, settings and Scamper's outfits are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Speech

When Scamper reads Spanish words aloud, it uses your device's built-in voice. The app does not use the microphone and does not record you.

Children

Scamper is suitable for learners of all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/Gjoel/scamper-ios/issues.
