**Description:**
Learn Latin American Spanish step by step with Scamper, a little golden dog with a red collar!

Travel north to south through 8 Spanish-speaking countries of South America: Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Paraguay, Chile and Argentina. Each country is a world with 50 levels, its own scenery, animals and an animal guide who cheers you on.

START FROM ZERO
Begin with the sounds of Spanish and the alphabet, then build up to everyday words, how words fit together, verbs, full sentences, real conversations, and finally the past and the future.

BITE-SIZED LESSONS
Every level teaches one small idea, then practises it with listening, matching pairs, building words and sentences from tiles, typing and reading aloud.

MINI GAMES WITH HAMP
Hamp, the extra-long greyhound in a sunflower hat, hosts bonus games: Odd one out, Match up, Speed match, El or la? and Unscramble.

EARN REWARDS
Win silver and gold coins, keep a daily streak, reach your daily goal and dress Scamper in hats, collars and more.

MADE FOR LATIN AMERICA
Real Latin American Spanish, with words like carro, jugo and celular, and fun facts and foods from every country.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and everything works without internet.
